Their journal shape, bounded retries, dual public routes, and the rule that a full log response at a client cap is not proof of completeness were inspected and followed. Those modules are not copied into this directory. Integer amounts are formatted by dividing base units by 10^18. No flapjax-probe expected totals are inputs.

The NodeReal URL is the [documented public shareable endpoint](https://docs.nodereal.io/reference/getting-started-with-your-api). It is not a personal credential. `nr_getAssetTransfers` is a public read on that same endpoint, with a stated range cap of 100000 blocks. No paid plan, account, signer, or new wallet is used.

Other read routes attempted in the capture are public JSON-RPC or unauthenticated HTTP. A route that fails is recorded. Failure is not treated as zero activity.

Chain quantities are observations. An address appearing here is not a claim of ownership, investment merit, award, or payment. A shared first BNB funder is an evidence group, not proof that one operator controls the recipients. Escrow booked on a task is not an award.
