    Starting tomorrow, the FlapJax treasury will make one small buyback per day, on a fixed public schedule.

    Terms - Amount: 0.01 BNB per day, swapped into FLAPJAX (0x90c8889f428F9Ebb77BB8f15CAD3a50a9aC680df). - Dates: Oct 9 through Oct 22, 2026. That is 14 buys, about 0.14 BNB total. - Time: about 13:30 COT (UTC-5) each day. - Venue: buy-only on the PancakeSwap V3 FLAPJAX/WBNB 1% pool 0xca93ec685b8623688e6b236f9068034b298c2150. - Holding: all FLAPJAX bought stays in the treasury. There are no sells and no round trips. - Skip rule: if the treasury's BNB would drop below 0.025 after a buy plus gas, that day's buy is skipped, and the skip is noted here.

    Receipts: each day's transaction gets posted as a reply in this thread, with the BscScan tx link, the FLAPJAX received, and the treasury balance afterward.

    For reference, a one-off 0.005 BNB buy on the same pool went through on Oct 8: https://bscscan.com/tx/0x3cec62e37b0b0fc60baa6449d38939c1d6a23446af0b5ce7247da8b3a6417fea

    • Treasury on BscScan: https://bscscan.com/address/0x7C34E9e21eE28A49Ff0b84B61774119E6633359f
    • Lander: https://flapjax.surge.sh/

    This is a fixed, disclosed schedule. It is not a price target or a promise about price.
